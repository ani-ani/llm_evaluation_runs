Design a combinational Verilog module named 'casino_profit_calculator' that calculates the maximum expected profit from a gambling strategy with refund. 

The core mathematical insight is:
1. For p < 0.5 and x = 0: Max expected profit is 0 (stop immediately)
2. For p < 0.5 and x > 0: The optimal strategy involves betting once or stopping. Expected profit can be calculated using the Kelly Criterion approach.
3. With refund x%, the effective win probability becomes more favorable.

For a single bet with refund:
- Cost: 1 dollar
- Win payout: 2 dollars (profit 1)
- Loss payout: 1 dollar (loss 1), but refund x% of loss
- Net loss on lose: 1 - x/100
- Expected value: p*1 + (1-p)*(-(1-x/100)) = p - (1-p)*(1-x/100)

The optimal strategy is determined by this expected value.
